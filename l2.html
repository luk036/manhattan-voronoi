L1 (Manhattan) vs L2 (Euclidean) Voronoi

This demo renders the same random sites under both metrics. The top row is Lee and Wong's L1 diagram and its dual Delaunay neighbour graph; the bottom row is the L2 diagram (Guibas and Stolfi's divide-and-conquer Delaunay triangulation) and its dual graph. The L1 graph is drawn from the neighbours reported by the L1 bisector graph; the L2 graph from the divide-and-conquer triangulation.

Refresh this page to generate another diagram. Add ?points=N to the URL to change the number of sites.
